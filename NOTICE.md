The controlling contract is docs/GDD-FINAL.md, approved 2026-11-08 Asia/Taipei. Adopted AICP 2.1.1 / UGDS 2.0.0 immutable source: https://github.com/jaychtw/game-development-standards/tree/61c24205f437826f1fc901f1741c49b677502f66 . No shared standard is modified.

PixelFX source: https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx/tree/a84159688af3ca37a4be05a0d422b94451b2e24e (0.3.0), MIT, Copyright 2026 Patch and Hugo Duprez. Selected transitive source files are retained in vendor/pixelfx/src with original source-level Sprite Fusion notices intact, alongside the complete MIT license. The separate gallery Patch Pixel font is excluded; all UI uses system fonts. Vendored core/font.js is the MIT procedural tiny bitmap data, not the excluded gallery font.

Direct integration: melee/thrust, melee/sword-slash, fire/fire-ring, moves/ice-lance-throw (charge/lance/burst sequence), lightning/chain-lightning, arcane/arcane-beam, holy/resurrection, critters/flying-skull. Modified chain accepts actual target coordinates; beam accepts its geometry length. Resurrection is time-compressed 4.8:1 into Blessing with a mask derived from the original game hero. The ice composed move supplies reference timing and source components; projectile collision and position replace the fixed gallery target. In released v1.0, rig/act metadata guided independently authored Roman and crowned warhammer sprites. The owner’s v1.1 amendment replaces those two implementations as described below. The background is original deterministic pixel geometry. These sources render at runtime: no opaque baking pipeline or missing export step.

Music and effects are original local Web Audio synthesis in src/audio.js. No audio downloads, services or runtime network are used. Source code and original procedural graphics/audio are under LICENSE (MIT); the original owner-supplied approved promotional PNG artwork is excluded from that code license, retained for this authorized project and portfolio publication only. No blanket third-party rights claim is made for the artwork.

Exact original artwork identity: docs/artwork/ARTWORK-MANIFEST.md and SHA256SUMS.txt. The repository contains the unmodified original PNG bytes. The card WebP derivative is made by scripts/cover.mjs without cropping, resizing or enlargement.

## v1.1 character reuse and adaptation

Hero: the actual, unmodified PixelFX demonstration character is rendered by `vendor/pixelfx/src/core/rig.js::posed`, using the original `act.js::poseAt`, `poses.js::READY`, and effect `act.poses` tracks. Its neutral shell, original magenta mark, construction, proportions and IK are retained. A uniform 1.5× nearest-neighbor presentation scale does not alter the rig or palette. No Roman helmet, red cape or blue armor customization remains in the runtime hero.

King: `src/characters/orc-rig.js` is a project-owned MIT derivative of that exact upstream rig: unchanged IK, bone frames, depth primitives, lighting/seams/outlining and pose cache; nonuniform torso/shoulder/head/limb anatomy; green skin, projecting jaw/tusks, depth-attached crown and fur mantle, red cloth, and a new spiked warhammer. `src/characters/orc.js` choreographs those joints with the original 12-fps pose interpolator and existing simulation clocks. It is not a palette swap, uniformly enlarged dummy or flat promotional sprite.

Original source-level notices remain intact. `act.js` additionally credits the MIT Hollow Crown timing/acting recipe, Copyright (c) 2026 rehan-remade. Full upstream MIT notice is retained in `vendor/pixelfx/LICENSE` and alongside the derivative in `src/characters/LICENSE`. Lineage hashes, scoped amendment, comparison fixture and verification are under `docs/v1.1/`. No excluded gallery font is imported. Spell code, geometry, duration and palette are untouched; only the actor mask used by the existing Blessing effect is necessarily derived from the replacement hero.
