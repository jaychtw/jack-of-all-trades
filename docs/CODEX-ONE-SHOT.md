## Full implementation (no intermediate owner gate)

Read the entire GDD before coding. Implement its **exact game**: fixed non-scrolling ruins, hero blue armor + red-crest Roman helmet + flowing red cape, Flying Skull minions one every 2s (exactly five per wave), one crowned green Orc King with heavy warhammer only after all five die, repeated identical waves. Hero 5 HP; skull 1 HP and contact 1, contact-location stop for 1s; boss 4 HP, telegraphed hammer 2 and 2s post-strike recovery; global 1s hit immunity. Hero always faces right, moves left/right across most of arena, cannot pass through enemies; no jump. Death -> Game Over -> Restart or Title; local best, gameplay-time survival and correct wave/kills reporting.

Deliver all seven combat actions with both animated character acting and accurate collision/damage:

- J: `melee/thrust`, light, 1, short front range.
- K: `melee/sword-slash`, heavy, 2, short front arc.
- 1: `fire/fire-ring`, 2 to **every enemy in its AoE**.
- 2: `moves/ice-lance-throw`, 2 only to **first collision**.
- 3: `lightning/chain-lightning`, 2 each up to **three distinct chained targets**.
- 4: `arcane/arcane-beam`, 2 each to enemies in forward beam at **end of >=3s**; hero stays rooted/action-locked for full beam.
- 5: `holy/resurrection` **adapted into Blessing**, approx **1s recognizable halo/wings/feathers/light cast**, complete invulnerability **5s from pressing**, free movement/attacks after casting, reactivation **refreshes** to five seconds without stacking or cooldown.

Exactly one active combat action, no queued input, hero rooted during active action; effects damage once per target per cast. Spells are unlimited and usable with zero nearby enemies. **Continual invulnerability by deliberate repeat Blessing is an approved feature, not a balance problem to solve.** Do not add resources, cooldowns, boss resistance, other enemies or difficulty progression.

Implement complete responsive title using original title PNG, with real usable accessible central START and fullscreen affordance aligned to the baked artwork; requesting fullscreen on START must never prevent play if denied. The game runs on the repository Pages subpath `/jack-of-all-trades/`; switchable fullscreen, visible UI pause/resume and P/Esc handling, automatically pause inactive tabs and portrait orientation and freeze all simulation timers; never “catch up” in background. Mobile landscape: **seven visible action buttons** and **two visible hold-to-move buttons**, no magic drawer. Keyboard A/D and arrows, J/K, 1–5, clickable combat buttons. HUD HP top-left, time/kills/wave top-center; Game Over time/kills/completed waves/local best. EN/zh-TW live toggle on title and in game without reset; appropriate accessible names. Sound effects **and** BGM, default on unless previously muted, one persistent mute toggle, browser autoplay graceful fallback. Local best/time/kills/waves and language/mute settings; graceful storage failure. Build original/appropriately licensed assets without third-party network or spending.
