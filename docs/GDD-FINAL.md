## 1. Product identity and boundaries

**Pitch.** A concise, atmospheric, dark-fantasy, pixel-art, single-screen survival game. A Roman-helmeted, red-caped sword-and-magic hero begins on the left of a ruined arena; flaming Flying Skulls emerge through a portal on the right. Kill five skulls to summon an imposing Orc King, defeat him, and repeat that identical wave forever. Survive as long as possible using two sword actions and five spectacular, deliberately unrestricted magic powers.

**Purpose.** A polished, immediately playable and replayable public game, and a practical proof of adapting PixelFX procedural character acting, sprites and effects into a cohesive original experience. Design and technical execution are deliberately modest: one arena, one minion type, one boss type, no outside game economy.

**Experience.** Readable, responsive, visually expressive combat; strong distinctive silhouettes; dramatic readable magical effects; short sessions, immediate restart and local best-time chasing. The magic system is intentionally a power fantasy, **not competitive balance**: invulnerability may be maintained by repeat Blessing. Do not nerf this behavior.

**Explicit non-goals.** No scrolling camera or multi-level world, jumping, platforms, different rooms, alternate enemy types, randomized modifiers, additional bosses, wave escalation, leveling, equipment, currencies, pickups, combo trees, mana, ammunition, cooldowns, account/login, online ranking, advertisements, external analytics, game server, multiplayer, purchasable content or automatic shared-standard updates. Only five specified spells and two sword attacks; do not add features merely because promotional art depicts extra orc warriors.

## 2. Core interaction loop and state model

1. **Title:** On initial load show the approved 16:9 title composition as a full-page responsive visual, not gameplay behind a splash overlay. Its large central START motif is an actually usable accessible/touchable button. Include a visible language switch and fullscreen control; no X/quit button. Controls/help may be shown compactly without obstructing artwork. The art's English logo remains a proper noun in either locale.
2. **START:** In that user gesture request browser fullscreen automatically, unlock user-gesture-gated audio, and start a fresh run. If fullscreen is unavailable, refused, or later exited, play normally in the landscape viewport instead. Never trap the player or require fullscreen to proceed.
3. **Arena:** Fixed side-view arena with ground plane, ancient ruins at dusk, a luminous right-hand portal. No horizontal or vertical world scrolling; game uses viewport-responsive scaling/letterboxing as appropriate. The hero moves horizontally across **most** of the traversable ground; boundaries keep him inside arena and out of the portal/spawn geometry. The hero **always faces right**. The hero and enemies are solid to one another; neither can pass through the other, and enemies cannot slip behind the permanently right-facing hero through collision tunneling.
4. **Wave:** Each wave schedules exactly **five Flying Skulls, one at a time, two seconds apart**. Skulls emerge from the right portal and pursue the hero. Multiple living skulls may coexist. No new minions spawn beyond those five during that wave. When **all five have been killed**, spawn **one Orc King**; never spawn him on a timer merely because five skulls have appeared. After boss defeat, count one completed wave, transition quickly to the next wave of five skulls, and repeat indefinitely with **identical difficulty** (no faster enemies, shorter intervals, extra health or new mechanics). Use a short, nonblocking transition if it improves clarity; no intermission menu or confirmation.
5. **Death:** Hero begins each run with five HP. At zero HP, stop the simulation and enter Game Over. Show survival time, enemies defeated, completed waves and local personal best; provide **RESTART** (immediately starts a new run) and **TITLE** (returns to title). No continuing the dead run and no loss of saved best or preferences on restart. No separate victory screen; beating a boss is the transition to the next wave.
6. **Pause:** Visible pause/resume control; P and Esc keys toggle when the browser delivers them (Esc may be intercepted by native fullscreen exit). A hidden/inactive document automatically pauses. Manual and automatic pause states must be reconciled safely: returning to a tab must never silently unpause a deliberately manual-paused run; show a clear Resume control. The paused simulation clock is frozen, including survival time, spawn schedules, movements, attacks, spell effects, Blessing and damage invulnerability expiration, wind-ups and boss recovery. No accumulated catch-up after tab inactivity. Exiting browser fullscreen must not terminate or reset the game.

## 3. Player, controls and combat state

**Hero visuals:** Original adapted PixelFX character, not merely an enlarged stock dummy: sleek slightly bluish metallic armor, Roman-centurion-style helmet with recognizable **red crest**, and flowing **red cape**, clearly readable at runtime pixel scale. His attacks must visibly synchronize body, sword, effects and recovery. The uploaded commercial reference helmet is inspiration for design language only: do not import watermarked/reference artwork or counterfeit its marks.

**Movement:** A/D and Left/Right arrows on desktop; two large, continuously pressable left/right buttons on mobile. There is no jump and no vertical player control. Holding movement moves until release or solid collision; movement is suspended while performing an active combat action. The hero's facing remains right even when walking left. Collision blocks passage, but movement control must never become stuck after touch-cancel, tab blur, pointer release or fullscreen change.

**Desktop combat:** J = Light sword, K = Heavy sword; 1 through 5 = spells in the order listed below. Seven visible clickable attack controls must also work with mouse and touch. Mobile landscape: exactly seven **visible** action buttons plus the two movement buttons, with legible/touchable grouping; do not replace magic with a hidden scrollable or expandable tray. Labels/icons and keyboard hints are readable without covering essential fight geometry.

**Action serialization:** Exactly one attack or spell can be active at a time. Hero is rooted for the **full action/casting animation** and cannot simultaneously sword-swing, cast, or walk. Inputs for additional combat actions during an active action are ignored, **not queued**. Source animation timings may guide implementation but gameplay effects and root duration must obey this GDD. A special effect must not persist after its owning action unless specified (exception: Blessing's explicit five-second protective status). Damage to each valid enemy occurs at most **once per cast/action**, not on every rendering frame. Actions may play and finish with no target, doing zero damage. No consumable resources, mana, cooldowns or implicit enemy-target requirement.

### Attacks and spells — complete required inventory

| Control | Exact PixelFX source ID | Damage to each valid target | Gameplay behavior |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| J / Light | `melee/thrust` | 1 | Short-range forward melee strike; valid enemies in its narrow forward hit area. |
| K / Heavy | `melee/sword-slash` | 2 | Short-range forward melee arc; valid enemies in that forward arc. |
| 1 / Fire Ring | `fire/fire-ring` | 2 | Circular area attack centered on hero; **every** enemy intersecting the ring's active hit area takes one hit. |
| 2 / Ice Lance | `moves/ice-lance-throw` | 2 | Rightward projectile; damages **the first** enemy intersected and then stops; does not pierce. |
| 3 / Chain Lightning | `lightning/chain-lightning` | 2 | Starts with nearest eligible enemy in the forward combat area and jumps to nearest distinct unhit valid enemies, **up to three total**; every one receives 2. Visual arcs follow actual target positions. |
| 4 / Arcane Beam | `arcane/arcane-beam` | 2 | Straight rightward beam; hero is rooted/action-locked for **at least three seconds**, then each enemy geometrically intersecting the beam **at the final damage moment** receives one 2-damage hit. Not instant-kill; no early damage. |
| 5 / Blessing | **Adapt** `holy/resurrection` | 0 | Cast visual approximately **1 second**, preserving light/halo/wings/feathers motifs; grants absolute invulnerability for **five seconds from button press**, including the cast. While casting, hero is rooted/action-locked; after casting, movement and attacks resume while the remaining protection persists. Recasting refreshes the expiration to five seconds from new press; never stacks time and has no cooldown. Continuous protection through repeated casting is intentionally allowed. |

All five named magical visuals are required and recognizable: do not replace them with generic flash circles. Use source-backed effects and adapt origin, scale, facing, collision, actual actors and layering for the game's engine. If no target is present, the appropriate full visual still plays with no damage. Combat collision calculations must derive from meaningful world hitboxes, not artwork alpha or giant invisible ranges. The abstract exact sizes, wind-up subframes, sprite pixel counts and minor visual tuning are delegated, provided attacks remain short/forward or appropriately spatial, readable and testable as specified.

**Timing note.** PixelFX source metadata describes some individual effect lengths rather than full acted character durations; do not assume they are interchangeable. Verified examples at upstream snapshot: thrust and slash effect durations 0.75s; Fire Ring 3.9s; Chain Lightning 1.8s; Resurrection 4.8s. **Explicit adaptations win:** Blessing cast ~1s (not original 4.8s) and Arcane Beam ~3s or longer even if upstream loops. Use the source acting/pose metadata proportionately and ensure damage timing visibly corresponds to impact.

## 4. Enemy specification and hazards

**Flying Skull (sole minion):** PixelFX `critters/flying-skull`, flaming skull, floating/bobbing while pursuing from the right. **1 HP**. Upon **actual contact** with the hero: deal **1 HP** unless hero currently invulnerable, then stop at its exact contact location for **one second** (no early precontact stop and no additional hit during that one-second pause), and then resume chasing. Avoid repeated one-frame contact collisions and movement tunneling; a skull may attack again only after completing recovery and having a legitimate subsequent contact event. Multiple skulls may overlap each other but must respect hero collision. Do not create conventional orc soldiers as additional minions.

**Orc King (sole boss):** One boss after all five skulls are killed. **4 HP**. Follow the approved promotional art's **green-skinned enormous crowned orc**, tusks/large mouth, bulky armored frame and shoulders, fur mantle, red cloth/cape accents, and oversized **spiked warhammer**. Build original sprite poses by adapting the PixelFX demo rig and/or original pixel art; a recolored or upscaled stock demonstration dummy is insufficient. Larger than hero, visually weighty, slower deliberate approach; boss body does not itself cause unspecified contact damage. When within effective melee distance, a **clear visible hammer wind-up** precedes strike; hammer causes **2 HP at actual impact** only if hero is in the strike area and not protected. After each strike he has **2 seconds of recovery** before another wind-up/attack. Do not add charge, shockwave, secondary projectile, new boss phases or unapproved attacks.

**Damage immunity:** After any successful hit, hero receives **one second of global damage immunity against all sources**. Skull and hammer attacks respect the same immunity, so simultaneous contacts do not multiply damage. Indicate hit and immunity visibly (e.g., flash), not solely by audio/color. Blessing supersedes damage entirely while active. Damage immunity is gameplay-timed and freezes during pause. Enemy HP decrement cannot go below zero or award multiple kills; dead enemies stop combat interactions promptly. Boss defeat increments the wave counter exactly once.

## 5. Interface, art and sound

**Screens and HUD:** Title -> arena -> pause overlay (reversible) -> Game Over -> restart/title. In arena: HP top-left; elapsed survival time, defeated count, and current wave status around top-center; mute, pause, fullscreen/exit fullscreen and live language switch in unobtrusive readable positions. Game Over shows actual elapsed game time, total defeated including bosses, **completed** waves, personal best, and its two buttons. Indicate controls at first play/on title without a forced tutorial. All key actions operable from keyboard/mouse and touch.

**Approved masters** shipped separately in the handoff archive; exact SHA-256 listed in `docs/artwork/ARTWORK-MANIFEST.md`:
- `docs/artwork/approved/title-screen-master.png` — approved title artwork, 1672×941. **It contains painted START text and a fullscreen icon**: these must be made **genuinely interactive** through aligned accessible controls, or faithfully integrated equivalent actual controls. Do not leave deceptive unclickable image controls. During Chinese localization, translate actual controls/accessibility labels; the approved English promotional title/logo and decorative lettering may remain as artwork. Preserve recognizable composition/characters and avoid cropping the central action or faces.
- `docs/artwork/approved/possibility-card-master.png` — separately approved promotional cover, 1672×941, no START, for Possibility > Games. Preserve composition/title lettering; generate correctly sized WebP delivery derivative without substituting another illustration. The art includes illustrative lesser orc warriors: these do **not** enlarge the gameplay enemy roster.

**Artwork hierarchy:** The original gameplay image is a **loose conceptual layout reference**: left hero, right portal/enemies, bottom row of action icons. Neither its exact ornaments nor its originally suggested scrolling nor its approximate eight squares are binding. The later approved title master and catalog-cover master establish **promotional compositions**, not game-sprite frames. Runtime gameplay must look like one cohesive, legible, atmospheric PixelFX-compatible pixel-art game, with animated original/adapted hero and king, discernible skulls, portal and grounded ruin environment. Do not place a giant static promotional illustration behind UI as a substitute for a functional arena; no placeholder rectangles or generic circles in place of character art. Preserve sharp pixel art, clear action telegraphs, distinguishable sprites, adequate visual contrast and the primary silhouette colors. Exact background decoration, rendered proportions, code architecture, effect particle counts and responsive positioning are implementation freedoms.

**Audio:** Both background music and responsive combat/UI sound effects; start enabled by default, except honor previously saved mute choice. A single visible mute/unmute toggle (persisted locally) affects all game audio. Respect browser autoplay/user gesture policies; audio denial or unavailable context must not prevent the game running. Use newly authored or properly licensed locally bundled/synthesized audio only, with readable attributions; no streaming music, unapproved music services, paid generators or trademarked soundtrack copies. Prefer sound that reinforces telegraph, hit, damage, boss and spell feedback; no essential information solely audio.

**Localization:** Full English and Traditional Chinese (zh-TW) for UI, controls/help, tooltips, pause, labels, status, sound control, failure/results and accessible names. Visible language toggle on title **and during gameplay**; instant switch without reset, lost time or altered combat state. The game name and embedded approved cover/title artwork lettering are not required to translate. The actual START function must be labeled accessibly and discoverably in both languages even if decorative raster art says START.

**Accessibility and responsive behavior:** Desktop browsers and mobile landscape. Landscape-only mobile game; portrait displays a bilingual rotate-device instruction and preserves game state (pause while unsuitable). If fullscreen request fails, remain fully usable in the landscape browser viewport. Test touch/emulation and keyboard/mouse. Show focus-visible, semantic buttons and labels, adequate hit targets (aim for >=44 CSS pixels or accessibility-equivalent on feasible devices), legible text, sufficient contrast, no reliance on color-only status, keyboard-operable core flow. Support prefers-reduced-motion by toning down nonessential parallax/shakes/flashes while preserving recognizable essential attack effects, telegraph and readable timing. Safe-area/viewport insets respected. Initial sound enabled but always easy to mute.

## 6. Persistence, technology, provenance and scope of delegation

**Save locally:** Longest completed-run survival time and its associated kills/waves; optionally update tie-best using more kills. Language and mute settings persist locally. No progress unlocks. Save only on completed runs (Game Over), not merely pausing or entering title. Corrupt/blocked localStorage must fail gracefully without breaking play; do not claim persistence when unavailable.

**Browser-only runtime:** Serve an entirely playable static client-side package under the repository GitHub Pages project subpath `/jack-of-all-trades/`. No backend, accounts, database/server, external APIs, runtime CDN dependencies, telemetry, secrets or personally identifying data. Game source and reproducible asset generation belong in the project repo. Offline service worker is optional, not required; at a minimum, once loaded, ordinary combat should not need an internet request. Correctly resolve all assets and routes under the project subpath.

**PixelFX dependency:** Upstream https://github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx, source snapshot commit `a84159688af3ca37a4be05a0d422b94451b2e24e`. Relevant selected IDs in Sections 3–4; procedural actor/rig in `src/core/actor.js`, `src/core/rig.js`, `src/core/act.js`, spritesheet export via `tools/act.mjs`. Upstream source/effects are MIT with copyright/notice requirements (Patch and Hugo Duprez, 2026; honor source-level borrowed MIT notices too). **Do not import `gallery/fonts/Patch Pixel`**, which is expressly not covered by upstream MIT. Review actual copied/imported files for license exceptions. Prefer pinned adapted effect code, spritesheets or a small integrated renderer; do not introduce a rendering library because it exists in a capability registry. All needed runtime assets must be bundled locally; $0 only. Log source commit, changes, retained notices and reproducible art pipeline.

**Codex decides:** Framework/renderer, game-loop scheduling, world-coordinate sizes, collision algorithms, within-spec timings that were not specified, collision margins, particles, responsive layout implementation, controls code, asset-export method, performance tuning, audio synthesis/source with valid license, tests, internal prototypes and checkpoints. Numeric defaults should preserve player readability and avoid unavoidable spawn/contact hits. Do not ask for routine owner input; use first-principles technical judgment and inspect actual running screens.

**Fallback order:** (1) adapt the exact verified PixelFX source effect/rig; (2) bake/export source-derived animation frames with preserved timing/visual identity; (3) reimplement technically equivalent visuals using original code/assets while maintaining specified silhouettes, source-inspired motifs and gameplay; (4) if those cannot satisfy frozen visual quality or functionality, stop the affected operation and report a narrow exception. For fullscreen failure, degrade to an ordinary responsive landscape viewport. For unsupported browser storage/audio, remain playable and present truthful state. For missing Pages/branch credentials, finish authorized code/PR, do not bypass or deploy elsewhere. **No fallback permits removal of a spell, boss, language, mobile support, approved promotional art or changing cost/public destination.**

## 7. Acceptance contract and test matrix

All must-pass behavior below is release-critical, unless a harmless platform-policy fallback was explicitly accepted above. Tests are proportionate under UGDS, not an arbitrary count target.

1. **First run:** Title artwork recognizable, central START usable through keyboard/mouse/touch; START requests fullscreen and starts the complete game even when fullscreen denied. Language toggle works on title and in-game. No exit X.
2. **Wave invariant:** Spawn exactly five flaming skulls per wave, two seconds apart, from right; multiple live skulls can exist; boss never appears before all five are dead; after one 4-HP Orc King dies, completed-waves increments once and the same five-plus-one cycle repeats without escalation or extra enemies.
3. **Combat inventory:** Both sword actions and all five distinct spells with exact IDs, correct 1/2 damage, actual AoE, first-hit projectile, maximum three chained, straight beam with *delayed* hit after >=3 seconds, and Blessing with ~1s recognizable cast and exactly 5s from press. Source-derived visuals work with actual hero/boss sprites, not gallery-only demonstrations. A hit only once per target per action; no-target casting still animates. Root/action lock/no queue validated.
4. **Combat edge cases:** Five hero HP; skull 1 HP and 1 damage only on real contact; skull stops *at* touch for 1s and cannot multi-hit during pause; boss 4 HP, hammer 2 damage at telegraphed impact with 2s recovery. Global 1s post-damage immunity, simultaneous collisions and Blessing protection correct; sprite/physics separation prevents passing enemies and spawn overlap. Unlimited spells and repeat Blessing protection are not nerfed.
5. **Gameplay state:** Pause/resume button, P and delivered Esc; inactivity auto-pause, return-to-tab safe resume; all simulation timers freeze and resume without catch-up. End at 0 HP, restart fresh, title navigation, retained personal-best and preferences, no ghost leftover enemies/events after restart.
6. **Display/input:** Visible seven combat buttons plus two mobile directional buttons; A/D, arrows, J/K, 1–5; actual working pointer/touch controls, including hold/release/cancel. Fixed landscape arena and correct portrait rotate-device pause/notice. HUD health top-left, time/kills/waves top-center; Game Over time/kills/completed waves/local best. User can leave/reenter fullscreen without losing the run.
7. **Localization/audio/storage:** Full English/zh-TW switching in a running encounter without reset; bilingual accessible labels. Music plus effect sounds, default enabled subject to saved mute, one mute toggle, playback-blocked fallback. Local best saved between reloads; storage unavailable/corrupt gracefully fails.
8. **Visual quality:** Inspect actual rendered title, ordinary combat with skulls, boss fight, at least one spell and Blessing, pause, game-over and two language modes at representative desktop/mobile landscape viewports. Compare title/card directly to approved PNGs and runtime visual criteria in §5. Pixel art must be coherent, sharp, correctly proportioned, with no unanimated generic enemy placeholders or obstructed controls. Record evidence; machine visual inspection is not an assertion of owner approval.
9. **Quality baseline:** Production build and relevant deterministic rules tests; browser representative happy path and actual death/restart path; touch and keyboard, narrow/wide landscape, reduced motion, targeted a11y inspection for critical controls and contrast; performance reasonably responsive on ordinary intended devices. No fixed arbitrary test-count requirement. Mark physical-device/human acceptance Not Verified if not actually exercised; those do not automatically create a second approval gate.
10. **Live release:** Versioned durable source and exact approved artwork masters present in project repository, hashes verified; proper upstream notices; protected-branch-compliant merge; public Pages URL serves running game with a real START/combat/restart interaction and no broken subpath assets. Possibility independently uses approved cover and verified Play URL; newest known-date Games sorting by current portal contract; current manual deploy gate respected; public entry and Play action verified independently. Never infer public state solely from commits, tests or queued Actions runs.
